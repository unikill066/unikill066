Overview

This policy explains how the personal portfolio website operated by Nikhil Nageshwar Inturi handles information when you visit the primary site at nikhil-inturi.vercel.app or its synchronized GitHub Pages mirror at unikill066.github.io/unikill066.

The site is primarily a static professional portfolio. It does not currently provide user accounts, a newsletter, advertising, or a working web-based contact form.

Information handled

Website visits. The hosting provider may automatically process technical request information such as an IP address, browser and device type, requested pages, timestamps, and diagnostic or security data. I do not use this site to independently build profiles about visitors.

Email communications. Contact links on the site use mailto: links. If you choose to email me, your message and contact details are handled by your email provider and mine. I use that information to respond and may retain the correspondence as reasonably needed.

Cookies and analytics. The portfolio does not currently set first-party analytics or advertising cookies and does not include an analytics service. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the new practice is represented here.

Hosting and third-party services

The site is hosted by Vercel, which may process request logs and related technical data under its own privacy policy.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. It also links to or can display content from third-party services, including GitHub, LinkedIn, Google Scholar, Medium, ORCID, Hugging Face, and project-hosting platforms. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. A third-party project preview is loaded only when you choose to launch it.

I do not sell or rent personal information collected through this portfolio.

Your choices

You can browse the main portfolio without submitting personal information. You can avoid loading an external project preview by not launching it, and you can configure your browser to limit third-party requests.

You may contact me to ask about information you previously sent by email, or to request correction or deletion where applicable. I may need to retain limited information when required for security, legal, or record-keeping purposes.

Children and policy changes

This professional portfolio is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it.

I may update this policy when the site or its services change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to inturinikhilnageshwar@gmail.com.
